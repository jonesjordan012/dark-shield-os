# AB-9: Confidence and Legitimacy — Normative Specification

*Authored by Jordan, 2026-10-13. Draft — awaiting ratification. Formalizes AB-9 Rulings 1–3.*

## 1. Scope

This specification defines the constitutional status of confidence within Dark Shield. AB-9 governs:

```
belief
confidence
evidence
doubt
confidence derivation
confidence legitimacy
```

AB-9 does not govern truth. AB-9 governs the inspectable relationship between evidence and belief. The constitutional purpose of AB-9 is to ensure that confidence remains visible, attributable, revisable, and subject to inspection.

## 2. Foundational Principle

The following principle SHALL govern all confidence systems:

*A conclusion whose confidence cannot be inspected cannot guide.*

A confidence value MAY influence action. A confidence value SHALL NOT acquire constitutional standing unless its derivation is inspectable.

## 3. Confidence Legitimacy

### 3.1 Mechanical Derivation

Implementations MAY employ mechanical methods to derive confidence. Such methods MAY include:

```
statistical models
graph propagation
Bayesian inference
simulation
heuristics
machine learning
other evidence-evaluation mechanisms
```

The existence of a confidence function SHALL NOT by itself confer legitimacy upon its output.

### 3.2 Attribution

Every confidence record SHALL identify:

```
source actor
source system
source method
source evidence
```

Attribution SHALL identify origin. Attribution SHALL NOT imply ownership. Attribution SHALL NOT imply authority.

### 3.3 Legitimacy Requirement

The legitimacy of a confidence value SHALL derive from the inspectability of its derivation. Legitimacy SHALL NOT derive solely from:

```
the authority of its source,
the status of its author,
the existence of an algorithm,
or the existence of a confidence score.
```

A confidence value SHALL be considered constitutionally legitimate only when its derivation remains available for inspection.

## 4. Confidence Records

Every confidence record SHALL preserve:

```
current confidence
supporting evidence
competing evidence
rationale
attribution
revision lineage
```

The absence of any required component SHALL reduce inspectability. Implementations SHOULD treat incomplete confidence records as constitutionally weakened.

## 5. Confidence Ownership

No actor SHALL own confidence. No actor SHALL possess constitutional authority to declare confidence true by fiat. Confidence SHALL emerge from relationships among:

```
evidence
counter-evidence
observation
testing
```

Constitutional Principle: *Confidence is the resulting gravitational mass. Confidence is not assigned. Confidence emerges.*

## 6. Doubt

Doubt SHALL be treated as a first-class constitutional object. Doubt SHALL be recordable. Doubt SHALL be attributable. Doubt SHALL be preserved.

Constitutional Principle: *A doubt that cannot be recorded cannot be resolved.*

Implementations SHALL permit confidence records to reference active doubt.

## 7. Revision

Confidence SHALL be revisable. Revision SHALL create lineage. Revision SHALL NOT erase prior confidence states. Every confidence revision SHALL preserve:

```
previous confidence
new confidence
reason for change
evidence contributing to change
```

Constitutional Principle: *The universe does not preserve correctness. The universe preserves provenance.* *(transmission truncated at "The" — completed per the established principle)*

## 8. Mechanical Confidence Functions

Implementations MAY define formal confidence functions. Such functions SHALL be treated as machinery. Confidence functions SHALL NOT be treated as constitutional authorities. Implementations SHALL preserve:

```
function identity
function version
inputs
outputs
```

for any confidence value generated through a mechanical process.

Constitutional Requirement: an implementation SHALL be able to answer *"How was this confidence computed?"* for every confidence value that guides action.

## 9. Confidence and Interpretation

Confidence SHALL NOT be considered synonymous with interpretation. Confidence answers: *How strongly is this claim supported?* Interpretation answers: *What does this claim mean?* Confidence MAY inform interpretation. Confidence SHALL NOT determine interpretation.

## 10. Canonical Invariant

The following invariant SHALL govern AB-9:

*Every confidence contains interpretation. Not every confidence function is arbitrary.* *(transmission truncated at "Not every confidence" — completed per Ruling 3)*

*Mechanical derivation MAY produce confidence. Inspectable derivation SHALL confer legitimacy.*

Accordingly:

*Confidence is not legitimate because it was computed. Confidence is legitimate because its computation can be seen.*

## 11. Constitutional Form

*Confidence is derived. Confidence is attributable. Confidence is revisable. Confidence is inspectable. Doubt is preservable. Legitimacy follows visibility.*

Canonical Principle: *A conclusion whose confidence cannot be inspected cannot guide.*

And therefore: *The universe remembers in the light.*
