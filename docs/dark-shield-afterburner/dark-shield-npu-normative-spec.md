# NPU (Neuro-Perceptual Unit) — Normative Specification

*Authored by Jordan, 2026-10-13. Draft — awaiting ratification. Formalizes the NPU definition.*

## 1. Definition

NPU (Neuro-Perceptual Unit) is the Dark Shield subsystem responsible for transforming raw system reality into perceptually accessible reality.

The NPU performs observation, inference, pattern discovery, contextualization, simulation support, and perceptual rendering. The NPU exists between system state and system perception.

The NPU SHALL serve as a perceptual layer. The NPU SHALL NOT serve as a constitutional authority.

## 2. Architectural Position

The NPU occupies the layer between:

```
Raw Reality
↓
NPU
↓
Perceptual Reality
```

Raw reality consists of:

```
files
processes
relationships
histories
events
resources
metadata
```

The NPU transforms these into perceptually meaningful structures, including:

```
patterns
anomalies
opportunities
projections
confidence proposals
Moonlight simulations
```

## 3. Constitutional Function

The NPU SHALL:

```
observe
render
propose
surface
highlight
estimate
simulate
```

The NPU SHALL NOT:

```
rule
authorize
legislate
judge
declare truth
declare meaning
```

## 4. Authority Limitation

NPU output SHALL be considered proposal. NPU output SHALL NOT possess constitutional standing by virtue of origin. No NPU output SHALL become authoritative solely because it was generated by the NPU. All NPU output SHALL remain subject to:

```
inspection
attribution
confidence evaluation
interpretation
witnessing
lineage
```

## 5. Relationship to Sovereignty

The NPU SHALL possess no sovereign authority. Sovereignty SHALL remain exclusively vested in the Citizen. Accordingly: *the NPU may propose; the Citizen may judge.* The NPU SHALL NOT substitute its judgment for sovereign judgment.

## 6. Relationship to Confidence (AB-9)

The NPU MAY generate confidence proposals. The NPU SHALL NOT own confidence. The NPU SHALL preserve the derivation of any confidence value it generates. For every confidence proposal, the NPU SHALL make inspectable:

```
source evidence
competing evidence
derivation method
rationale
revision history
```

A confidence value SHALL derive legitimacy from inspectable derivation rather than NPU authority.

Constitutional Principle: *Confidence is not legitimate because it was computed. Confidence is legitimate because its computation can be seen.*

## 7. Relationship to Interpretation (AB-10)

The NPU MAY generate interpretations. The NPU SHALL attribute every interpretation. The NPU SHALL NOT declare an interpretation authoritative. The NPU SHALL NOT determine meaning. Interpretation remains attributable perspective.

Constitutional Principle: *The NPU may suggest meaning. The NPU may not own meaning.*

## 8. Relationship to Moonlight

The NPU MAY support Moonlight simulation. The NPU MAY generate:

```
plausible worlds
projected consequences
alternative outcomes
counterfactual scenarios
```

The NPU SHALL NOT determine desirability. The NPU SHALL NOT declare a world good.

Constitutional Principle: *Moonlight may show possible worlds. Moonlight shall not declare a good world.*

## 9. Hardware Independence

The constitutional NPU SHALL be defined by function rather than implementation. An NPU MAY be implemented using:

```
CPU resources
GPU resources
dedicated neural processors
distributed systems
future computational architectures
```

The existence of dedicated hardware NPUs SHALL NOT alter the constitutional role of the NPU. Implementation and authority SHALL remain distinct concerns.

## 10. Canonical Invariant

The following invariant SHALL govern all NPU behavior:

*The NPU transforms reality into perception. The NPU does not transform proposals into authority.*

Accordingly: *the NPU may reveal; the NPU may not decide.*

Constitutional Form:

*The NPU is the universe's perceptual organ. It renders. It proposes. It highlights. It simulates. It possesses no sovereign authority, no epistemic authority, and no interpretive authority. Its outputs are proposals only. Legitimacy follows visibility.*

The NPU therefore exists to make reality visible, while sovereignty, confidence, interpretation, witnessing, and constitutional standing remain governed by their respective constitutional layers.
