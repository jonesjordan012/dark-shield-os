## Why this matters

The Copyright Office requires human authorship. AI-assisted works are registrable, but the application must describe what the human created and explicitly exclude AI-generated material that is more than de minimis. Omitting it can invalidate the registration later. (Source: Copyright Office, *Copyright Registration Guidance: Works Containing Material Generated by Artificial Intelligence*.)

## What to enter

**1. "Author Created" field** (on the Authors screen — this is your claim):

> Constitutional rulings, schema design, validation rules, status derivations, and selection, coordination, and arrangement of the seven documents as a single unit.

**2. "Material Excluded"** (Limitation of Claim screen):

> Text passages and JSON schema definitions generated by artificial intelligence (Meta's Muse assistant) at the author's direction, review, and approval.

**3. "New Material Included"** (Limitation of Claim screen):

> Human-authored rulings, designs, and expression; selection, coordination, and arrangement of all seven documents into the single-unit work.

**4. "Note to Copyright Office"** (optional, paste-ready):

> This work was created by the author, Jordan Jones, with the assistance of an artificial intelligence tool (Muse, an AI assistant by Meta). The author directed the AI's contributions, reviewed and revised them, and ruled on every constitutional question they touched. Passages drafted by the AI and JSON schema definitions it completed at the author's direction are excluded from the claim above; the claim covers the author's own expression and his selection, coordination, and arrangement of the seven documents as a single published unit.

## Notes

- Do not list the AI tool or its maker as an author or co-author.
- If any field doesn't seem to fit what you want to say, the Office's own guidance says you may simply state that the work contains AI-generated material — the examiner will contact you to work out the details.
- Keep this file with the filing package. It's part of the paper trail: the registration says what the specification itself demands — provenance, preserved.
