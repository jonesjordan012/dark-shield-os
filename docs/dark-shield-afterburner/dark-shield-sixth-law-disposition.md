# Proposed Sixth Law — Disposition

*Ruled by Jordan, 2026-10-13. Canon.*

*Confidence is visible.*

## Status

Disposition: **Deferred**
Constitutional Status: **Already embodied in canon.**
Independent Law: **Not ruled.**

## Findings

The principles underlying the proposed Sixth Law are fully implemented within the published constitutional framework governing confidence, derivation, attribution, doubt, revision lineage, and inspectability. The existing AB-9 doctrine already requires that confidence remain attributable, revisable, inspectable, and historically preserved. The published confidence model further requires preservation of:

```
evidence
counter-evidence
rationale
attribution
revision lineage
confidence history
```

A confidence value therefore acquires constitutional legitimacy through visible derivation rather than numerical assertion. The resulting framework is consistent with the broader Dark Shield commitment to visible relationships, visible history, and visible traces embodied by Temporal Echoes and relationship-based structures.

## Rationale

The proposed law is correct as a statement of principle. However, the constitutional framework has established a stronger doctrine. The proposed law states: *Confidence is visible.* The implemented doctrine states: *Confidence is visible as history.* Visibility of a score alone is insufficient. Constitutional visibility requires visibility of:

```
what was believed
why it was believed
what challenged it
what changed
why it changed
what is believed now
```

Accordingly, the substance of the proposed law is already present within canon at a more precise and operational level.

## Constitutional Interpretation

The proposed Sixth Law SHALL be interpreted as shorthand for the following established doctrine:

*Confidence is visible not as a number, but as a history.*

## Future Consideration

If the Dark Shield Laws are later expanded beyond ontological principles into explicit epistemological principles, the proposed Sixth Law MAY be reconsidered for independent adoption. Should such adoption occur, it SHOULD be understood as a concise expression of existing AB-9 doctrine rather than as creation of a new constitutional principle.

## Final Disposition

*The proposed Sixth Law is law-worthy. The proposed Sixth Law is not presently necessary. Its substance already exists within canon. No additional ruling is required.*
