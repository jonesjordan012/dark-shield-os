# The All Systems Go Echo — how AB-8 is audited

*Draft v0.1, 2026-10-11. Read-authority ruled by Jordan 2026-10-12 — the open tail is closed; the rest remains a proposal built from his rulings.*

## The principle

Two of your rulings converge here:

- **Constitutional sovereignty:** "The sovereign may suspend the constitution, but never in the dark."
- **Governance through visibility:** "The deeper the access, the more lit the room."

So the audit mechanism for AB-8 is not a gate — nothing blocks the Citizen's hands. It is a **witness**. The room remembers everything done inside it.

## What the Echo Recorder captures — the sovereign echo

When the Citizen engages All Systems Go, the Temporal Echo Recorder opens a **sovereign echo**: a single record of the session, write-once, signed by the NPU as truthful, that the Citizen cannot edit or delete. A sovereign who cannot hide.

The echo holds four things:

1. **The perceptual record** — *what the Citizen saw.* The prosthesis state: time made evident, the hallucination rendered on the UI, the critical metrics visible, Dark Shield seen as protectorate. This answers: in what light did they act?
2. **The deed trace** — *what the Citizen did.* Every bare-metal command, its target (stars, pulsars, galaxies, anchors, the paradigm system), and its effect. The deepest possible trace for the deepest possible access — nothing touched leaves no mark.
3. **The plausibility context** — *why the hands moved.* The NPU-designated plausible twinkling star mind map pattern this action served. Or, in catastrophic recovery, the flag **"recovery — no pattern, universe broken"** — because break-glass has its own honesty, and the record must say which kind of All Systems Go this was.
4. **The anchor delta** — *what the anchors looked like before and after.* The Citizen can reach beneath the Reality Anchors, so the anchors' trusted history records the touch — including the fact that the touch bypassed them. The anchor is not silent about being bypassed. The bypass itself becomes part of trusted history.

## Why this closes the audit question

Question 3's first half was already ruled: the visibility principle supplies the **perceptual record** (what the Citizen saw). This draft supplies the second half: the **durable echo log**. And it is the mechanism behind your sovereignty facet — the anchors "bind even the Citizen" not by blocking the hands, but by **remembering everything the hands touched**. Constraint by memory, not by lock.

## Honest limit

The echo records what was seen and done — not the Citizen's *why*. The why stays human. That's by design: the NPU governs perception, the echo preserves history, but the judgment that moved the hands belongs to the sovereign alone. The record is honest about what it cannot hold.

## The ruling: Sovereign Echo Read Authority (Jordan, 2026-10-12)

Jordan ruled deliberately narrow — five points, codified almost verbatim:

1. **The Citizen always has read access.** The Echo exists partly to constrain the Citizen through memory. *A record you cannot inspect cannot bind you.* The Citizen may read every field of every Sovereign Echo at any time.
2. **Reality Anchors have trusted-history access.** The Anchors may read the Echo for integrity, continuity, promotion, audit, and historical reconstruction. They do not own the Echo; they preserve it as part of trusted history.
3. **No ordinary process may read it by default.** Galaxies, pulsars, agents, services, and applications receive no implicit access merely because they are running. Access must be explicitly delegated through a citizen action or an anchor-mediated export.
4. **Moon Universes never receive direct access.** A Moon may receive a copy, excerpt, or redacted projection — never the authoritative Sovereign Echo itself. The authoritative record belongs to the trusted history of the Sun Universe, consistent with the Moonlight trusted/experimental split.
5. **The Echo itself is never secret from history.** If an AB-8 event occurs, the existence of the Echo and its anchor delta become permanent historical facts. What happened can be examined later. Why it happened remains the Citizen's burden unless separately recorded.

**Sovereign Echo Access Rule (canon):**
*The Citizen shall always possess unrestricted read access to every Sovereign Echo. Reality Anchors shall possess read access sufficient to preserve trusted history, verify continuity, and record anchor deltas. No other entity shall possess inherent read authority. Access by agents, services, galaxies, applications, or Moon Universes requires explicit delegation by the Citizen or a derived historical export authorized by the Anchors. The authoritative Sovereign Echo shall reside only within trusted history.*

The principle, in one line: **the Citizen owns the experience. The Anchors preserve the truth. Everyone else sees only what is intentionally shared.**

This ruling also settles the edge of AB-8 open question 1 (anchor relationship): the anchors are witnesses and preservers of the sovereign act — never its owners, never its gatekeepers.
