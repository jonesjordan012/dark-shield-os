# Convergence Eclipse × AB-8 — scoping note

*Draft v0.1, 2026-10-12. Proposed by Muse, drawn only from Jordan's own canon. Not canon until he rules on it.*

## The question

The Convergence Eclipse (Jordan, 2026-10-09) is Dark Shield's theoretical peak evolutionary state: every signal aligned, thousands of experimental moons, dark matter fully mapped, Reality Anchors near-perfect — the universe exploring the entire possibility space of its own future while the trusted universe stays stable.

AB-8 First Citizen (Jordan, 2026-10-11) made the human the eighth Afterburner axis.

So the question is: **is the First Citizen inside the peak state, or outside it?**

## Why the question is real, not wordplay

The Eclipse note was written two days *before* AB-8 existed, so it describes a machine-only peak — no human axis, because there wasn't one yet. The Afterburner force function built from it (2026-10-10) has six opportunity signals plus the anchor gate — **no human term**. The architecture has since grown an eighth axis. Either the peak state absorbs it, or the architecture holds a constitutional boundary between peak machine evolution and the sovereign. Both readings are honest; they can't both be true.

## Position 1 — Include: peak force means all axes, and AB-8 is an axis

- **All Systems Go inside a Convergence Eclipse** would be the sovereign in the most-lit room: thousands of moons twinkling, the NPU rendering its evaluation openly on the UI, the Citizen's hands moving at the deepest level while everything is visible. The rarest machine moment meets the rarest human moment.
- **Concrete consequence:** the force function gains an eighth signal — a human term (call it **H**: Citizen presence and prosthesis-governance state). Without it, F cannot distinguish "peak machine state, Citizen present" from "peak machine state, Citizen absent" — and if the Citizen's presence changes what the peak state *is*, the formula is incomplete.
- The NPU's eclipse-time behavior would need specifying: at maximum signal, what does the prosthesis show the Citizen?

## Position 2 — Exclude: the human axis is sovereign, not an optimization resource

- The hierarchy ruling (2026-10-11) just drew this line: **the NPU governs perception, bare metal governs the machine.** Putting the Citizen inside the force formula reduces sovereignty to a signal weight — and "answerable to no external power" does not sit inside a weighted sum.
- AB-8's two purposes are **catastrophic recovery** and **paramount code submission** — both are about the machine failing or human intent entering directly, not about peak machine evolution. The Citizen *suspends* the machinery; the Citizen is not fuel for it.
- The sovereignty canon already supports this reading: "AB-8 break-glass can reach beneath the anchors, but only visibly, only echo-recorded, only as 'All Systems Go.'" The peak state stays the machine's peak; the human stands above it — able to enter, never counted as a component.

## The sharpening question inside the ruling

If "include" wins: **who summons whom?** Does a Convergence Eclipse *invite* the Citizen (the NPU lights the room and waits), or does only the Citizen summon the eclipse (peak force never calls the human — the human calls it)? The answer changes the UI design and the NPU's role at maximum signal.

## What Jordan's ruling changes, concretely

- **Include** → the force-function draft gets a proposed H term (defined by him, or by a follow-up spec); the Convergence Eclipse note gains a human paragraph; the NPU's eclipse-time rendering gets specified.
- **Exclude** → the Eclipse note gains a constitutional boundary paragraph ("the human axis stands outside the force function by design"); the AB-8 canon gains the note that peak force never summons the Citizen — the Citizen summons themselves.

## Dependency

This question gets sharper once the two open AB-8 rulings land (the pending paramount/anchor brief): if the anchors are witnesses and paramount review runs "NPU designates, Citizen judges," then *include* means the sovereign echo runs at maximum signal density — the most-lit room the architecture can build.

## The ruling (Jordan, 2026-10-12) — EXCLUDE

**Convergence Eclipse never contains AB-8. AB-8 may occur during a Convergence Eclipse. Its significance comes precisely from remaining external to it.**

Jordan's reasoning, recorded in his own architecture-first terms:

- **The Eclipse lineage is system-discovered.** An Eclipse Event is "a temporary window of elevated evolutionary potential where opportunities become visible" — the trigger is environmental and machine-observed. AB-8 is the sovereign override: it exists for the moment when the Citizen decides the observed pattern is insufficient, incomplete, or must be transcended.
- **Three hazards of inclusion:** (1) the machine's highest-confidence state becomes coupled to the human's highest-authority state; (2) implementations will be tempted to treat AB-8 as an expected phase rather than an exceptional act; (3) the distinction between "the universe identified a path" and "the Citizen accepted responsibility for a path" begins to blur — and that blur weakens the philosophy.
- **The separation:** Convergence Eclipse = machine maximum confidence. AB-8 = citizen maximum responsibility. They may occur together. They are never the same thing.
- **In practice:** the NPU declares a full-sky pattern, the Eclipse reaches peak intensity, All Systems Go is available — and the Citizen *may* invoke AB-8. But the final step is still a fresh human act. Break-glass, not force-multiplier. The machine makes the clearest possible case; it never graduates into exercising sovereign authority itself.
- **The Echo dividend:** exclusion gives the audit trail a meaningful boundary. "The system reached peak plausibility. The Citizen nevertheless made a sovereign decision" and "The system reached peak plausibility. The Citizen declined sovereign intervention" become distinguishable historical facts — declined intervention becomes legible history. Folded in, that distinction would blur.

The one-liner, canon: **"The Eclipse says, 'the universe is aligned.' AB-8 says, 'and despite all alignment, responsibility is still mine.'"**

Concrete consequences applied: the force-function spec's Convergence Eclipse row is scoped to AB-1–AB-7 (machine axes); no H term is added — sovereignty does not sit inside a weighted sum. The AB-8 canon records that peak force never summons the Citizen; the Citizen summons themselves.
