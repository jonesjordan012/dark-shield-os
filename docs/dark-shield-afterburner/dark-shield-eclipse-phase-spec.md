# Dark Shield Eclipse-Phase Specification

*Authored by Jordan, 2026-10-13. Normative Draft v0.1 — awaiting ratification. Formalizes the first-boot narrative.*

## 1. Purpose

The Eclipse Phase is a temporary operational state during which possibility becomes unusually visible and additional perceptual assistance becomes available to the Citizen. The Eclipse Phase exists to support exploration, adaptation, optimization, recovery, and cognitive resilience.

## 2. Constitutional Principle

*During Eclipse, possibility becomes visible.*

The Eclipse Phase SHALL NOT alter sovereignty. The Citizen SHALL remain sovereign during Eclipse. Eclipse increases perception. Eclipse SHALL NOT increase authority.

## 3. Entry Conditions

An Eclipse MAY be initiated when:

```
elevated possibility is detected
significant uncertainty is detected
optimization opportunities accumulate
Twinkling Stars exceed threshold
Moonlight demand exceeds threshold
user-requested
```

Implementations MAY define additional entry mechanisms. All Eclipse initiations SHALL be recorded by Echo.

## 4. Eclipse Countdown

The universe SHALL expose an Eclipse Timer. The timer SHALL remain visible prior to Eclipse initiation. The timer MAY display:

```
time-to-eclipse
gravity trends
antigravity trends
possibility density
twinkling concentration
hallucinated metrics
projection
```

Metrics generated by the NPU SHALL be clearly attributable. The timer SHALL function as temporal prosthesis.

Constitutional Interpretation: *The timer prepares. The timer does not command.*

## 5. Eclipse Environment

Upon Eclipse initiation:

```
visual intensity MAY increase
possibility rendering MAY increase
Twinkling Star visibility MAY increase
Moonlight accessibility MAY increase
prosthetic support MAY increase
```

The universe SHALL remain navigable. Applications SHALL remain launchable. Normal operation SHALL continue. Eclipse SHALL augment interaction. Eclipse SHALL NOT replace interaction.

## 6. The Dark Shield

The Dark Shield is an Eclipse-only assistance layer. The Dark Shield SHALL appear during Eclipse. The Dark Shield operates as a resilience subsystem. The Dark Shield SHALL NOT operate as a governing subsystem.

The Dark Shield SHALL:

```
surface alternative approaches
offer recovery paths
identify refuge options
generate assistance code
highlight overlooked possibilities
support cognitive resilience
```
*(transmission truncated at "support cognitive" — completed per §1's purpose)*

The Dark Shield SHALL NOT:

```
override sovereign decisions
execute actions autonomously
determine meaning
determine truth
```

## 7. Refuge Doctrine

The Dark Shield SHALL function as constitutional refuge.

Canonical Analogy:

*The Command Module is normal operation. The Lunar Module is the Dark Shield.* *(transmission truncated at "is" — completed per the first-boot canon)*

The Dark Shield SHALL serve the role of survivable operation. The Dark Shield exists primarily for moments when ordinary pathways become inadequate.

Constitutional Form: *The Dark Shield is not the pilot. The Dark Shield is the refuge.*

## 8. Prosthetic Assistance

During Eclipse, prosthetic systems MAY increase sensitivity. Prostheses MAY provide:

```
attention metrics
focus metrics
task metrics
navigation metrics
pattern metrics
hallucinated metrics
possibility metrics
```
*(final list item transmitted as "possibility" — completed to "possibility metrics" per the list pattern)*

Prosthetic outputs SHALL remain attributable. Prosthetic outputs SHALL remain proposals. Prosthetic outputs SHALL NOT acquire authority solely by appearing during Eclipse.

## 9. Hallucinated Metrics

Hallucinated metrics SHALL be permitted. For purposes of this specification: *hallucinated metric = a non-literal exploratory metric used to reveal relationships, possibilities, or perspectives.* Examples MAY include:

```
anti-time
possibility density
gravitational tension
planetary accessibility
trajectory confidence
```

Hallucinated metrics SHALL remain attributable, inspectable, and distinguishable.

Constitutional Requirement: hallucinated metrics SHALL NOT be represented as objective fact. They SHALL be represented as exploratory perception.

## 10. Eclipse and Moonlight

The Eclipse Phase SHALL increase access to Moonlight operations. During Eclipse, Moonlight MAY:

```
generate possible worlds
simulate interpretations
explore counterfactual paths
evaluate consequences
```

Moonlight SHALL NOT determine desirability.

Constitutional Principle: *Moonlight may show possible worlds. Moonlight shall not declare a good world.*

## 11. Eclipse and Echo

All Eclipse interactions SHALL remain Echo-eligible. The following MAY generate Echoes:

```
user selections
confidence revisions
Moonlight explorations
Dark Shield interventions
prosthetic observations
```

Eclipse SHALL increase visibility. Eclipse SHALL NOT reduce traceability.

## 12. Exit Conditions

An Eclipse SHALL end when:

```
timer expires
possibility density falls below threshold
Citizen exits Eclipse
system-defined completion criteria are satisfied
```

Exit SHALL generate an Eclipse Echo. That Echo SHOULD preserve:

```
entry conditions
significant events
confidence changes
Moonlight outcomes
Dark Shield assistance
resulting lineage
```

## 13. Canonical Invariant

The following invariant SHALL govern Eclipse behavior:

*Eclipse increases perception. Eclipse does not increase authority.*

Accordingly:

*The NPU may reveal. The Dark Shield may assist. Moonlight may explore. The Citizen remains sovereign.*

Constitutional Form:

*Standard Phase reveals reality. Eclipse reveals possibility. The Dark Shield appears when possibility must become survivable. It does not rule. It does not decide. It does not replace the Citizen. It helps the Citizen continue.*

Canonical Principle: *The Dark Shield is not the authority. The Dark Shield is the lifeboat.*

## §14. Formal State Transition Model — Dark Shield Eclipse Lifecycle

*(Authored by Jordan, 2026-10-13.)*

This section defines the normative operational state machine governing Eclipse behavior, Dark Shield activation, Moonlight accessibility, prosthetic amplification, and Echo generation.

### 14.1 State Set

The universe SHALL occupy exactly one primary operational state at any point in time.

```
BOOT
STANDARD
PRE-ECLIPSE
ECLIPSE
AFTERGLOW
```

### 14.2 State Definitions

**BOOT** — Persistent formation state. Initial starscape construction. Universe topology generation. Relationship discovery initialization.

Properties:

```
Citizen access: enabled
Moonlight: disabled
Dark Shield: unavailable
Prosthesis: baseline
Echo generation: enabled
```

Exit Condition: universe initialization complete. Transition: `BOOT → STANDARD`.

**STANDARD** — Normal navigational state. Applications, galaxies, stars, pulsars, and visible relationships remain accessible. Twinkling Stars may accumulate. Antigravity may increase.

Properties:

```
Citizen sovereignty: full
Moonlight: limited
Dark Shield: inactive
Prosthesis: baseline
Eclipse timer: visible
```

Permitted Transitions: `STANDARD → PRE-ECLIPSE`, `STANDARD → STANDARD`.

**PRE-ECLIPSE** — Possibility accumulation phase. NPU intensifies observation. Timer becomes increasingly expressive. Twinkling concentration increases. Antigravity visibility increases.

Properties:

```
Citizen sovereignty: unchanged
Moonlight: preparing
Dark Shield: inactive
```

Required Outputs — timer MAY display:

```
time-to-eclipse
possibility density
gravitational tension
anti-time
trajectory metrics
hallucinated metrics
```

Exit Conditions: eclipse threshold reached → `PRE-ECLIPSE → ECLIPSE`; possibility dissipates → `PRE-ECLIPSE → STANDARD`.

**ECLIPSE** — Enhanced possibility state. Primary exploration phase. Moonlight access expands. Dark Shield appears. Prosthetic assistance reaches maximum amplification.

Properties:

```
Citizen sovereignty: unchanged
Moonlight: expanded
Dark Shield: active
Prosthesis: maximum
Possibility visibility: maximum
```
*(transmission truncated at "Possibility visibility:" — completed per the state pattern)*

Required Behaviors — the system MAY:

```
surface hidden relationships
propose confidence revisions
identify Twinkling Stars
generate Moonlight scenarios
provide Dark Shield assistance
```
*(final item transmitted as "provide" — completed per the Eclipse state definition)*

The system SHALL NOT:

```
override Citizen decisions
create sovereign acts
assign truth
assign meaning
```

Constitutional Requirement: *Eclipse increases perception. Eclipse does not increase authority.*

**AFTERGLOW** — Post-Eclipse consolidation phase. Results become lineage. Possibilities become history. Confidence revisions stabilize. Echoes finalize.

Properties:

```
Citizen sovereignty: unchanged
Moonlight: reduced
Dark Shield: withdrawing
Prosthesis: returning to baseline
```
*(transmission truncated at "Prosthesis:" — completed per the consolidation pattern)*

Required Behaviors — generate Eclipse Echo containing:

```
entry conditions
significant interactions
confidence changes
Moonlight outcomes
Dark Shield interventions
resulting lineage
```

Exit Condition: stabilization complete. Transition: `AFTERGLOW → STANDARD`.

### 14.3 Secondary State Overlays

Overlay states MAY coexist with any primary state.

**DARK_SHIELD_ACTIVE** — activated only during Eclipse (`ECLIPSE + DARK_SHIELD_ACTIVE`). Entry: eclipse begins. Exit: eclipse ends. Purpose: provide refuge-oriented assistance. Constraint: *Dark Shield may assist. Dark Shield may not govern.*

**MOONLIGHT_ACTIVE** — may occur during STANDARD, PRE-ECLIPSE, ECLIPSE, AFTERGLOW. Constraint: *Moonlight may simulate. Moonlight may not decide.*

**PROSTHESIS_AMPLIFIED** — occurs when additional perceptual support is available. May expose: focus metrics, attention metrics, cognitive metrics, trajectory metrics, hallucinated metrics. Constraint: *Prosthesis reveals. Prosthesis does not command.*

### 14.5 Forbidden Transitions

The following transitions SHALL NOT occur directly:

```
BOOT → ECLIPSE
BOOT → AFTERGLOW
STANDARD → AFTERGLOW
PRE-ECLIPSE → AFTERGLOW
```

A completed Eclipse cycle SHALL always pass through:

```
STANDARD → PRE-ECLIPSE → ECLIPSE → AFTERGLOW → STANDARD
```

### 14.6 Echo Requirements

The following transitions SHALL generate Echoes:

```
PRE-ECLIPSE → ECLIPSE
ECLIPSE → AFTERGLOW
AFTERGLOW → STANDARD
```

Echoes SHOULD preserve:

```

Dark Shield activity
prosthetic activity

```

### 14.7 Canonical Invariant

The state machine SHALL preserve the following invariant:

*Possibility may expand. Perception may expand. Assistance may expand. Authority shall not expand.*

Accordingly: *The NPU may reveal. Moonlight may explore. The Dark Shield may assist. The Citizen remains sovereign.*

Constitutional Form:

*BOOT forms the universe. STANDARD reveals the universe. PRE-ECLIPSE anticipates possibility. ECLIPSE exposes possibility. AFTERGLOW preserves possibility. The Citizen remains sovereign throughout.*

And therefore: *The universe remembers in the light.*

## §15. Afterburner System

*(Authored by Jordan, 2026-10-13.)*

### 15.1 Definition

Afterburner is the Eclipse-phase acceleration subsystem. Afterburner exists to increase the rate at which possibility can be explored. Afterburner does not alter sovereignty. Afterburner increases exploration. Afterburner SHALL NOT increase authority.

Constitutional Principle: *Eclipse reveals possibility. Afterburner accelerates possibility.*

### 15.2 Activation

Afterburner MAY activate only during `ECLIPSE`. Direct transition from `STANDARD → AFTERBURNER` SHALL NOT occur. Activation requires:

```
Active Eclipse
+
Detected Twinkling Stars
+
Available possibility density
```

### 15.3 Afterburner Clusters

An Afterburner Cluster is a temporary concentration of evolutionary potential. Clusters form around:

```
Twinkling Stars
active galaxies
repeated user attention
optimization opportunities
Moonlight
```

Clusters SHALL be visually distinct from ordinary stars. Clusters MAY exhibit:

```
increased brightness
orbit instability
gravitational distortion
possibility trails
anti-gravitational effects

Interpretation: a cluster does not indicate correctness. A cluster indicates: *something may be worth exploring.*

### 15.4 Afterburner Hallucinations

Afterburner Hallucinations are exploratory perception artifacts generated during Afterburner operation. For purposes of this specification: *Afterburner Hallucination = a generated perception intended to reveal unexplored relationships and possibilities.* *(transmission truncated at "intended" — completed)* Examples MAY include:

```
future trajectories
unseen dependencies
counterfactual relationships

Constitutional Requirement — Afterburner Hallucinations SHALL be attributable, SHALL be inspectable, SHALL remain distinguishable. *(transmission truncated at "SHALL remain" — completed per §9)*

### 15.5 Planetary Functions

```

process consolidation
resource rebalancing

Planetary Functions SHALL operate within the current reality.

### 15.6 Anti-Planetary Functions

Anti-Planetary Functions operate against assumed structure. Their purpose is to challenge certainty. Examples MAY include:

```
assumption inversion
counter-workflows
contradictory hypotheses
alternative classifications

Constitutional Principle: *Planetary functions optimize. Anti-planetary functions destabilize assumptions. Neither SHALL be considered authoritative.*

### 15.7 Relationship to Moonlight

Moonlight explores:

```

Afterburner explores:

```

Moonlight asks: *What if this world existed?* Afterburner asks: *What if this world changed?* The two systems MAY cooperate. Neither SHALL determine desirability.

### 15.8 Relationship to the Dark Shield

The Dark Shield acts as refuge. Afterburner acts as accelerator.

Distinction — *Dark Shield: survivability. Afterburner: exploration.* The Dark Shield helps the Citizen continue. Afterburner helps the Citizen discover.

### 15A.2 Cluster State Machine

```
DORMANT → IGNITING → ACTIVE → DECAYING → ARCHIVED
```
*(diagram reconstructed from the collapsed transmission; all five states defined below)*

- **DORMANT** — possibility exists but has not yet crossed Eclipse visibility threshold.
- **IGNITING** — possibility concentration increasing; twinkling activity accelerating.
- **ACTIVE** — cluster contributes directly to Afterburner exploration.
- **DECAYING** — possibility exhausted or explored.
- **ARCHIVED** — historical cluster retained through Echo lineage.

### 15A.3 Cluster Metrics

**Gravity** — measures attachment to existing reality. High gravity indicates: existing workflows, established structures, high usage.

**Antigravity** — measures unrealized possibility. High antigravity indicates: unexplored approaches, latent opportunities, counterfactual pathways.

**Orbit Instability** — measures potential for change. High values indicate: structural tension, contradictory patterns, competing forces. *(transmission truncated at "competing" — completed)*

**Mass** — measures accumulated significance. Inputs MAY include: citizen attention, echo lineage, confidence history, usage concentration.

### 15A.4 Possibility Metrics

**Possibility Density** — how much unexplored possibility exists inside the cluster. Range: 0.0–1.0.

**Transformation Velocity** — rate of potential change. High values indicate rapid evolution potential.

**Novelty Pressure** — pressure exerted against existing structure; how strongly the cluster argues for change.

**Emergence Index** — likelihood of generating something new: new workflow, new interpretation, new relationship, new optimization.

### 15A.5 Planetary Metrics

Planetary metrics describe pressures acting within accepted structure.

**Planetary Force** — optimization pressure inside current reality: refactor suggestions, workflow simplification, performance improvements.

**Anti-Planetary Force** — pressure against assumed reality: inverted workflow, counter-hypothesis, unexpected classification, alternative meaning.

Constitutional Principle: *Planetary functions optimize. Anti-planetary functions challenge.*

### Editorial Note (Jordan, 2026-10-13)

The paired examples are intentionally contrasted. A cluster dominated by gravity and planetary force indicates pressure toward refinement of an existing structure, while a cluster dominated by antigravity and novelty pressure indicates pressure toward departure from that structure. Both outcomes are expressed through the same measurement schema; only the direction of accumulated change differs. The differing confidence values are likewise illustrative: optimization signals tend to be more readily supported by present evidence than innovation signals, consistent with the expectations of AB-9. Afterburner Clusters describe where change is gathering, not where change is guaranteed to occur.
